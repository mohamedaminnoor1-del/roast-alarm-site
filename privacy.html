Roast Alarm

Privacy.

Effective October 12, 2026

The short version
  • Roast Alarm doesn't collect your data. We have no accounts, no servers and no analytics, and we don't track you.
  • Your alarms, stats and friends' recordings stay on your phone.
  • Apple handles subscriptions. We never see your payment details.

This policy explains what Roast Alarm (“the app”, “we”) does with your information.

What stays on your phone

Everything you create in the app is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Alarms and settings: times, repeat days, labels, the characters you pick, and your snooze settings.
  • Wake-up history: when you tapped Snooze or I'm up. It powers the “Your week” stats. The app deletes entries older than about 30 days.
  • Friend recordings: Secret Roasts and Sabotage, recorded on your phone with the microphone. The audio is cleaned up and saved on your phone only. Deleting a recording in the app removes it.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Apple may include this data in your backup, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only so a friend can record a roast on your phone. The microphone is used only while the recording screen is open. Recordings never leave your device.

Alarms and notifications

The app uses Apple's AlarmKit so alarms ring on time, even in Silent mode or a Focus. Alarm details are handled by iOS on your device.

Subscriptions

Roast Alarm Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We don't receive your name, email or payment details. The app only checks, on your device, whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the app opens the iOS share sheet with a short message. You choose where it goes, and nothing is shared unless you send it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking tools.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal information.

Children

Roast Alarm contains strong language and is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future feature works differently, we'll update this page and change the date above. For example, roasts written by AI would need to send information off your phone. We'll ask for your permission in the app before any of your information leaves your device.
