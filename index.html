Support.

Roast Alarm wakes you up with a roast. Here's help when something isn't working.

Email us: roastalarm.help@gmail.com

My alarm didn't ring

Open Roast Alarm and check the alarm is on. Then go to iPhone Settings → Roast Alarm and make sure Alarms are allowed. Alarms ring even in Silent mode and Focus, but not if the phone is switched off.

I can't hear the countdown voice in the Timer

The spoken countdown plays while the app is open, or if you lock the phone during the countdown. The alert at zero always rings.

Subscriptions

To manage or cancel Premium, go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Roast Alarm Premium. If you bought Premium on another device, open the Cast tab → See plans → Restore Subscription.

Privacy

Your alarms and recordings stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
